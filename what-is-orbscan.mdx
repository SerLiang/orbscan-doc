---
title: "What is Orbscan?"
---

An on-chain activity explorer for Polymarket prediction markets.

[**Orbscan**](https://orbscan.com/) is an on-chain activity explorer for [**Polymarket**](https://polymarket.com/) prediction markets. It reads events directly from the chain and exposes them through a small JSON API, so you can:

- Look up a trader’s **current portfolio** of open positions and USD holdings.
- Pull a trader’s **full activity history** — every buy, sell, and redeem across every market they’ve touched, paginated.
- Decode any **transaction hash** into a human-readable summary and the individual actions it contains.

There’s no indexer to run and no on-chain data to parse yourself — Orbscan has already done that work.
