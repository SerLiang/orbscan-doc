Whether you're building a copy-trading dashboard, running performance analysis, or simply tracking your own PnL, the Orbscan API puts the full depth of Polymarket data at your fingertips.

## Get up and running in three steps

New to Orbscan? Follow this path to go from zero to your first live data response.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your API key">
    Create a free account at [orbscan.com](https://orbscan.com), navigate to **Settings → API Keys**, and generate your personal key. Your key authenticates every request you make. See [Authentication](/authentication) for full details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your first request">
    Pass your key in the `Authorization: Bearer` header and call any endpoint — for example, fetch a trader profile by wallet address. The [Quickstart](/quickstart) guide walks you through the exact request and response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the full API">
    Dig into leaderboards, live market activity, copy-trading simulations, deposit history, and more. The [API Reference](/api-reference/overview) documents every available endpoint with parameters, response schemas, and example payloads.
  </Step>
</Steps>
